Study Title:

Implicit Confidence in Web-based Gesture Ratings

Purpose:

This study explores whether hand gesture characteristics can reveal implicit confidence in rating tasks.

Procedure:

  • Phase 1 — Gestures: Respond to questions with natural hand gestures
  • Phase 1 — Your meaning: Label the same questions with explicit buttons
  • Phase 2 — Gesture spectrum: Show gestures across a spectrum (chill → hesitant)
  • Your hand movements will be tracked using your webcam
  • The study takes approximately 15-20 minutes

Data Collection:

We collect: gesture type, movement features (speed, smoothness, duration), 21-point hand landmarks, and your explicit ratings. All data is anonymized.

Privacy:

Your webcam is used for real-time hand tracking and video recording for research purposes only.

📖 Instructions

📝 Phase 1 — Gestures

Read each question and respond with a natural hand gesture. Hold briefly until you see "Detected — thank you."

📝 Phase 1 — Your meaning (no camera)

You'll now see the same questions again. Choose Very good / Good / Neutral / Bad / Very bad for each one.

📝 Phase 2 — Gesture spectrum

For each prompt (from "really chill" to "very hesitant"), show that feeling with a gesture. Hold briefly until "Detected — thank you."
